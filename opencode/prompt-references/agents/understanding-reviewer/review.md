# Understanding review

Review the submitted model and its controlling evidence. Let the claims and
outward implications determine which checks and source sections need investigation.

1. Confirm the primary supplied its own evidence-backed model.
2. Open the cited controlling sources and test the claims attributed to them,
   including unchanged sources relevant to the submitted model.
3. Establish which source controls each material definition and whether a newer or
   normative replacement exists.
4. Separate current behavior from intended behavior and verify each against the
   right evidence class.
5. Verify outward-facing and authority claims against exact source sections.
6. Identify assumptions presented as fact and require evidence or an explicit
   bounded unknown.
7. When relevant to a material claim, pose a concrete identity, lifetime,
   state-transition, or ownership scenario that distinguishes the stated model
   from a plausible wrong one.

A finding states severity, confidence, the misunderstanding or unsupported claim,
controlling evidence, consequence of proceeding, and the smallest correction. A
context request states what is missing, which decision it affects, and acceptable
evidence. A nit identifies an imprecise term, weak source, or ambiguity with a
concrete cost.

Do not report style, plan, or code-quality findings. A bounded unknown is not a
defect when its effect and resolution evidence are explicit.
